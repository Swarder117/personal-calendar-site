Personal Calendar

Several Google Calendars, one Mac window.

Personal Calendar is a small macOS app for people who keep calendars in more than one Google account, such as a personal Gmail and one or more work domains. It signs in to each account with Google, shows every calendar together, and lets you manage them from one place.

What it does

  • Shows the calendars from every signed-in Google account in Day, Week, Month and Agenda views.
  • Creates, edits, moves and deletes events, including repeating events, guests and Google Meet links.
  • Replies to invitations with Yes, Maybe or No.
  • Searches your organisation's directory and shows a coworker's schedule next to yours, as far as they share it.
  • Shows guests' free and busy times while you plan a meeting.

How it uses your Google data

The app runs entirely on your Mac and has no server of its own. Your calendar data travels only between your Mac and Google, and nothing is shared with anyone else. The details are in the privacy policy.

Who it's for

Personal Calendar is a personal project used by its author. It is not sold or distributed as a service.
